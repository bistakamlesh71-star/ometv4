🛡️ NEPAL X ESPORTS — OFFICIAL RULES

1. 🚫 No Hack, No Panel

  • No hacks, scripts, mod APKs or third-party cheating tools.
  • No panel, config abuse or unfair gameplay advantages.
  • Fair play is compulsory for everyone.

2. 🤝 Respect All

  • Respect all players, team members and admins.
  • No abuse, hate speech, bullying or personal attacks.
  • No toxic behaviour or unnecessary arguments.

3. 🎮 Fair Play

  • No teaming with opponents in solo matches.
  • No bug abuse or exploiting game glitches.
  • Play honestly and follow tournament rules.

4. 🏆 Tournament Rules

  • Follow the instructions given by tournament admins.
  • Use the correct in-game name and UID.
  • Do not use another player's identity.
  • Fake results or screenshots are not allowed.
  • Admin decisions must be discussed respectfully.

5. 📵 No Spam

  • No repeated messages, fake announcements or advertising without permission.
  • Do not disturb tournament matches or other players.

6. 🔐 Account Safety

  • Never share your password or OTP.
  • Do not impersonate NEPAL X ESPORTS staff.
  • Report suspicious activity to the admins.

7. ⚖️ Punishment

  • Rule violations may result in a warning, match disqualification, temporary suspension or removal from the team, depending on severity.
  • Cheating may lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Admin decisions must be based on evidence and applied fairly.

🔥 PLAY FAIR. RESPECT ALL. REPRESENT NEPAL. 🇳🇵
